What types of premises do you clean?

We offer cleaning for offices, warehouses, stores, schools, medical and dental clinics, and construction sites. Tell us about your premises and the work you need.

How often can you clean?

Daily, weekly, fortnightly and one-off cleaning options are available. We will discuss a frequency that fits your premises and priorities.

Is the assessment or quote free?

Yes. Contact us for a free assessment or quote. Share your location, type of premises and cleaning needs to help us understand the job.

Can the service fit my budget?

Tell us your priorities and budget. We will discuss a suitable scope and frequency so you can make an informed decision.

How do I raise a concern?

Call or email us with the details. Open communication is part of our approach, and we aim to respond as soon as reasonably possible.

How does the enquiry form work?

The form prepares an email draft. Review it, then send it through your email app, or copy it into an email to nononsenseservicesptyltd@gmail.com. The form does not send your message automatically.
