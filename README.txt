MAIN WEBSITE — VERSATILE PROPERTY SERVICES
Approved black, gold and ivory design; polished 13 October 2026.

HTML, CSS, JavaScript and locally bundled Bootstrap and Lenis.
Open index.html after extracting this ZIP, or upload its contents to a static host.
Pages: Home, About, Services, Accreditations and Contact.
Styles load in this order: Bootstrap, style.css, elegance.css, Lenis, scroll-motion.css, polish.css.

ENQUIRIES
No Formspree or other submission service is connected. The form validates and prepares a draft for review. Visitors open their email app and send it, or copy the draft into an email. The website does not automatically send or store enquiry details. Existing contact phone and email links work independently of the form.

MOTION
Smooth wheel scrolling, native touch scrolling, progressive section reveals and a five-second photo slideshow. Reduced-motion preferences are respected. The slideshow suspends off-screen and in hidden tabs. No navigation dots or pause buttons are displayed, as requested.

CONTENT
Business details and existing photography come from the client's website. Certification categories were checked against https://www.versatilecleaning.com.au/ on 13 October 2026. Current certificates and their scope should be supplied by the client on request. No invented metrics, awards or client ratings are used.

LAUNCH HANDOVER
This is a preview hosted on the existing Main Website URL. Before moving to the client's domain, update canonical links, Open Graph URLs, sitemap.xml and robots.txt to that final domain. Confirm business addresses, service scope, image rights and current certification with the client. Agree any privacy/legal pages with the client; the existing terms link leads to their current website. Hosting, domain ownership and email submission service are separate setup decisions.

CHECKS
JavaScript syntax, page/resource links, fragment targets, unique IDs, form choices, image decoding and stylesheet structure checked. Enquiry review, email generation and copy handling exercised with a simulated DOM. Browser visual and real-device interaction testing was not available in this environment and remains a launch check.
