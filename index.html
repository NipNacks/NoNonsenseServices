What should I include in my enquiry?

Start with your suburb, property type and approximate floor area. Include your preferred cleaning frequency, access hours and any areas that need particular attention.

Can I enquire about a one-off clean?

Yes. Use the enquiry form for a one-off task or an ongoing cleaning requirement. Describe what needs attention so the team can discuss a suitable scope.

Can cleaning fit around our opening hours?

Include your preferred times and any access restrictions in your enquiry. The team can discuss availability and agree a schedule with you before work starts.

How is the price determined?

The scope, size and condition of the property, cleaning frequency, access and specialist requirements all affect the quote. A tailored enquiry helps the team understand the work involved.

Can I request certification documents?

Yes. Ask the team for current quality, safety and environmental management certificates, including the scope and validity details relevant to your supplier review.

How does the enquiry form work?

The form prepares an email draft using the details you enter. Review and send it in your email app, or copy the enquiry and email it to customerservice@versatileservices.com.au. Completing the form alone does not send a message.
